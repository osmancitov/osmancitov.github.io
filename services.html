Services

I am an engineer and technician who takes on small technical jobs for people in the United States. I work alone, I keep the scope small, and I say plainly what I can and cannot do.

What I do

  • Web pages and small sites. Plain, fast, readable pages in HTML and CSS, hosted on GitHub Pages or on the host you already use. No heavy frameworks unless the job needs one.
  • Repositories and small tools. Cleaning up and maintaining a Git repo, fixing broken links and builds, writing scripts and small command-line tools, keeping documentation in step with the code.
  • Technical writing and translation. READMEs, guides, short reports and web copy in clear English or Spanish, and translation between Spanish and English. Code blocks, commands and quoted text are kept exactly as they are.

How a job works

  • Send me a short description of what you need and what you already have.
  • I reply with what I would do, how long I think it will take, and the price before I start.
  • Work is delivered through your repo or as plain files, so you can read every change.
